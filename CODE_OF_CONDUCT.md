# Code of Conduct

KeebWeaver contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Respect differing experience levels, accessibility needs, and hardware
  constraints.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and outcomes rather than people.
- Protect other people's private information and device data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety and supported hardware scope.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate privacy
violations, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct problems privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary. This policy applies in all project
spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
